Private Pay Practice

At this time, Bridge Speech Therapy operates as a private pay practice and does not bill health insurance directly. Payment is due at the time service is rendered.

Operating as private pay allows us to dedicate 100% of our focus to your child's individual needs without restrictions or limitations on session counts, locations, or diagnostic categories imposed by insurance companies.

Insurance Superbills

If you wish to seek reimbursement from your health insurance company for speech therapy, we can provide you with an itemized monthly statement called a Superbill.

Superbills contain standard diagnostic (ICD-10) and procedure (CPT) codes that you can submit directly to your insurance provider for out-of-network benefits reimbursement.

Tip: We recommend calling your insurance company prior to starting therapy to ask about your out-of-network speech therapy coverage.
⚖️

Good Faith Estimate & Rights Under the No Surprises Act

Under Section 2799B-6 of the Public Health Service Act, health care providers are required to inform individuals who are not enrolled in an insurance plan or coverage, or individuals who are not using insurance, of their right to receive a "Good Faith Estimate" of expected charges for non-emergency medical items and services.

  • You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services.
  • Make sure your healthcare provider gives you a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your medical service or item.
  • If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill.
  • Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate.
