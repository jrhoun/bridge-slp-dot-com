Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. How we protect your personal and website information.

1. Introduction

Bridge Speech Therapy ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of parents, guardians, and visitors to our website (www.bridgeslp.com). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to ensure your information remains safe.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Voluntary Information: When you submit a contact form, request a consultation, or schedule an appointment via Calendly, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, child’s age/grade, and any general inquiry details you provide.
  • Automated Website Data: We collect non-personally identifiable technical information (such as anonymized IP addresses, browser types, device categories, referring pages, and visit timestamps) using standard web analytics to help improve our site’s functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through this website is used solely to:

  • Respond to consultation requests and parent inquiries.
  • Schedule and coordinate initial speech-language evaluations or therapy sessions.
  • Analyze website performance and visitor navigation patterns to enhance user experience.
  • Comply with applicable state and federal legal obligations.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or your child’s information to any third parties or advertising networks under any circumstances.

5. Healthcare Privacy & Clinical Information

Please note that general inquiries sent through our public website contact form are intended for initial scheduling and consultation requests. Formal clinical records, medical histories, and Protected Health Information (PHI) created once client care commences are maintained securely in compliance with applicable healthcare confidentiality and HIPAA guidelines.

6. Analytics & Cookies

Our website uses first-party cookies and privacy-hardened web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics) to measure site traffic. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without restricting your access to our site.
