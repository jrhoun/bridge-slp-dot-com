Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about in-home therapy, service areas, insurance, and evaluations.

Bridge Speech Therapy provides convenient in-home speech therapy in parts of the Central Los Angeles area, including West Hollywood, Beverly Hills, Culver City, Santa Monica, Silver Lake, Echo Park, Hancock Park, and surrounding neighborhoods. For families residing outside these areas or anywhere in California, we provide virtual Telehealth speech therapy services.

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance companies directly. However, we can provide you with an itemized monthly Superbill that you can submit to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement. We accept HSA/FSA cards, credit cards, Zelle, and checks.

Yes, each new client must have a formal speech-language evaluation before treatment can begin. This allows us to accurately diagnose areas of need, set baseline metrics, and create tailored goals. If your child has completed a comprehensive speech evaluation by a licensed SLP within the past 6 months, we may be able to waive this requirement upon review of the report.

Session lengths vary based on your child's age, attention span, and specific therapy goals. Standard therapy sessions are offered in 30-minute, 45-minute, or 60-minute blocks. The recommended frequency and session length will be discussed following the initial evaluation or consultation call.

Parent involvement is essential to a child's progress! Depending on your child's age, parents can participate directly in sessions or join during the final 5–10 minutes for review, hands-on demonstration, and discussion of home practice activities.

We specialize in pediatric speech and language development, working primarily with infants, toddlers, preschoolers, and school-aged children through elementary school.
