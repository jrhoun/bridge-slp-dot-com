Does my child need help?

Every child develops speech and language skills at their own pace, but early intervention makes a significant difference. If you are wondering whether speech therapy could benefit your child, we are here to support you.

  • ✓ Hard to understand compared to peers of the same age
  • ✓ Frustrated when trying to express thoughts, needs, or wants
  • ✓ Difficulty following directions or understanding instructions
  • ✓ Gestalt language processing or repeating phrases/echolalia
